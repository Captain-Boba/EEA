    {{ t("Definitions and coverage notes") }}
    • {{ t("Generation, electricity mix, consumption and carbon intensity come from Ember’s curated monthly and annual data.") }}
    • {{ t("Prices are national monthly Ember values and are weighted by calendar days for complete years.") }}
    • {{ t("Gross electricity imports and exports are annual Eurostat values. Negative-price hours and operational interval data are outside the scope of this monthly Atlas.") }}
    • {{ t("Net imports are positive for net importers and negative for net exporters. The net import share uses consumption from the same period.") }}
    • {{ t("Eurostat metrics and derived per-capita, capacity-factor and EV values are only available in the annual view.") }}
    • {{ t("The theoretical nominal EV battery capacity uses a flat fleet assumption of 60 kWh per BEV. It represents neither usable energy nor V2G or grid-available storage capacity.") }}
    • {{ t("EEA CRT 1.A.1.a covers public electricity and heat production and is not an electricity-only figure.") }}
    • {{ t("GDP ratios use nominal GDP at current prices. Long-term comparisons may therefore be affected by inflation and changes in price levels.") }}
    • {{ t("Estimated generation emissions are calculated from carbon intensity and generation. A positive decarbonisation rate means carbon intensity fell compared with the previous year.") }}
    • {{ t("Storage values are separately dated inventories, not monthly or annual flows. German battery data comes exclusively from Battery-Charts; other batteries and all pumped storage come from JRC.") }}
    • {{ t("The JRC hydropower inventory is a separate plant snapshot. Only directly reported storage energy is displayed; missing energy remains missing.") }}
    • {{ t("The current month is provisional. The current year is reported as YTD using completed months only.") }}
    • {{ t("A blank value means the specified source provides no metric for that country and period.") }}
